# Auxiliary / Focus Head Losses — Training-Only Steering

> How heads that are dropped at inference steer the shared encoder. Surveyed Sep 2026.

## 0. Core mechanism — why dropping works
- **Shared-backbone gradients only:** `L = L_main + Σ λ_i L_aux,i`; `∇_shared` from aux reshapes encoder features; aux head params discarded at test. No inference cost.
- Canonical pattern: Deeply-Supervised Nets (DSN) — companion SVM/conv classifiers per hidden layer, joint `output + Σ aux` loss for transparency, discriminative early features, anti-vanishing [Lee et al. 2014](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.5185); GoogLeNet subsidiary classifiers; CNDS gradient-threshold placement (`mean-grad <1e-7` after 10–50 warmup epochs) [Wang et al. 2015 via review](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1902.10030).
- Modern template: DSR-Net adds 4 intermediate decoder seg heads + lightweight recon head at highest-res feature, **removed at inference**, leaving main encoder-decoder-head [Li & Jiang, Vis. Comput. 2026](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00371-026-04563-2); BCAR-Net recon decoder **removed at inference**, no test-time compute [BCAR-Net, Plants 2026](https://www.mdpi.com/2223-7747/15/12/1762).
- KD template: teacher affects only weights, not forward pass [PyTorch KD Tutorial](https://docs.pytorch.org/tutorials/beginner/knowledge_distillation_tutorial.html); Dual-Head Self-Distillation for YOLO11 — lightweight aux neck+head learns from main head, **only main head at inference** (+0.8 AP COCO, +1.0 minitrain, +0.9 VOC) [Tamura & Todo, Neurocomputing 686, 2026](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0925231226011471).

## 1. Auxiliary reconstruction decoder as regularizer
- **Attach:** lightweight decoder `g_φ(h(x))` at hidden/fused feature, parallel to main path; train jointly, prune for transfer/inference [Cui et al. 2024 survey](https://www.emergentmind.com/topics/auxiliary-reconstruction-loss); NAR extension branches from encoder parallel to processor-decoder to force encoder to retain input-state info [NAR+Recon 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.00559v1).
- **Targets/losses:** input, side-info, density map; L2 / BCE / SSIM+L1; dual-head (seg + recon) in Prior-SegNet [Ernst et al. 2022]; RGB+depth recon from same fused feature `L_count + L_recon-RGB + L_recon-depth` [BCAR-Net 2026](https://www.mdpi.com/2223-7747/15/12/1762).
- **Effect:** forces “common information” in latent → data-efficiency, robustness to shift/scarcity; Table 12: full 7.92 MAE vs 8.98 w/o recon; depth-recon matters more than RGB for geometry [BCAR-Net 2026](https://www.mdpi.com/2223-7747/15/12/1762); multi-task + deep-supervision improves gradient flow, 1–5pt gains for +10–200% train params [EmergentMind Deep Supervision](https://api.emergentmind.com/topics/deep-supervision).
- **Design rules:** hidden features for generality, outputs for label-structured recon; small bottlenecked decoder so aux regularizes not dominates [Cui et al. 2024]; two-stage curriculum — recon-pretrain then joint/final-task, or Stage1 recon-heavy + weak main, Stage2 joint with epoch-decay `γ(t)` [BCAR-Net 2026](https://www.mdpi.com/2223-7747/15/12/1762); phased recon→joint in FPD [Li et al. 2020].
- **Risks:** trivial/mean memorization → partial collapse if unbalanced; not invariant to pixel-permutation/sensor remap (prefer latent-prediction/contrastive there) [Voelcker et al. 2024 via survey](https://www.emergentmind.com/topics/auxiliary-reconstruction-loss); over-regularization with misaligned aux [Li et al. 2022].

## 2. YOLO-style 1D detection transplanted to intervals
- **YOLO triad to copy:** `λ_cls·L_cls + λ_box·L_box + λ_dfl·L_dfl` — BCE objectness (obj present? 1/0), CIoU box regression, classification BCE/focal/VFL + DFL distribution over bins [YOLOv11 Loss & Training](https://aegean.ai/aiml-common/lectures/scene-understanding/object-detection/yolo/pytorch/04_loss_and_training/04_loss_and_training); YOLOv5/7 CIoU, YOLOX IoU, YOLOv6 SIoU/GIoU+Varifocal, YOLOv8 CIoU+DFL [LearnOpenCV YOLO Loss](https://learnopencv.com/yolo-loss-function-siou-focal-loss/); objectness = BCE vs target [Educative YOLO Loss](https://www.educative.io/courses/deep-dive-into-object-detection-with-yolo/understanding-loss-calculation).
- **CIoU:** `L_box = 1−CIoU(b̂,b*)` unifies overlap + center distance + aspect ratio; converges faster/better than Ln/IoU/GIoU, esp. from poor init [Zheng et al. AAAI 2020 / TCYB](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.08287) ([code](https://github.com/Zzh-tju/CIoU)); use as train loss, mAP for eval [ML Metrics CIoU](https://karam-nus.github.io/metrics/docs/metrics/object-detection/ciou).
- **1D mapping:** box `[x1,y1,x2,y2]` → interval `[c−l/2, c+l/2]`; 1D-IoU = intersection/union; center offset `c=(c_gt−c_unit)/l_unit`, span `s=log(l_gt/l_unit)` + `smooth-L1(c)+smooth-L1(s)` on pos (+incomplete) proposals; completeness hinge + cls + loc `L=L_cls+αL_comp+βL_loc (α=β=0.1)` [ETP Temporal Action Localization 2018](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1804.04803v1.pdf); replace smooth-L1 with `1−1D-CIoU/DIoU` (center-distance / enclosing-length + length-consistency) as 2D YOWO-variant did `smooth-L1→CIoU` 91.26→92.26 frame-mAP [Sci. Rep. 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-73622-2).
- **Existence proofs:** YOLOv3-1D — 2D→1D convs, predicts bounding *domains* (center/length/objectness/class), mAP@50-95 0.99 on Gaussian/Lorentzian/skew peaks & steps [ljbkusters/YOLOv3-1D](https://github.com/ljbkusters/YOLOv3-1D); YOLO1D — YOLOv8-inspired 1D-CNN for time-series classification+localization [milliyang/yolo1d](https://github.com/milliyang/yolo1d); temporal-action-localization benchmarks (THUMOS14/ActivityNet) are the video analogue [Awesome-TAL](https://github.com/Alvin-Zeng/Awesome-Temporal-Action-Localization).
- **As aux focus head for TSAD:** supervise on injected-probe intervals, then drop; gives localization bias to encoder without test-time NMS cost; handle extreme bg/fg imbalance with focal loss [Lin et al. ICCV 2017](https://learnopencv.com/yolo-loss-function-siou-focal-loss/) + hard-negative mining (1:4 pos:incomplete, top-¼ incompletes) [ETP 2018](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1804.04803v1.pdf); anchor-free center/length + objectness simplest for 1D.

## 3. Attention / steering supervision (FiLM sparsity, entropy)
- **FiLM:** `γ,β = f_c(x), h_c(x)`, `FiLM(F|γ,β)=γ⊙F+β` — scale/shift/negate/shut-off per channel, threshold with ReLU; 2 params/channel, cost ∝ channels not resolution [Perez et al. AAAI 2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.07871) ([code](https://github.com/ethanjperez/film)); steering use: L1 on `γ` for channel sparsity, or aux loss pulling `γ,β` toward probe masks; sensitivity `γ≫β` [FiLM survey 2025](https://www.emergentmind.com/topics/feature-wise-linear-modulation-film).
- **Entropy sparsity:** `L = L_c + λL_a`, `L_a=−Σ α̂_i log_L α̂_i` (low=sharp); supervised variant on `β̂=sigmoid(a)` with Jaccard to avoid bg bias; finding: marginal plausibility gain, risks deleting plausible tokens / wrong focus; deeper context harms plausibility [Nguyen et al. 2023/2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.12775v1).
- **Modern recipes:** per-head learnable threshold `θ_{l,h}` + tolerance margin, `L_total=CE+λ·penalty(H−θ)`; global `-mean-H` to broaden (EAR debias: subgroup-AUC 70.97→80.08) vs `+mean-H` to sharpen; differentiable cluster/range surrogates [Jha et al. 2025; Attanasio et al. 2022; Shihab et al. 2025 via](https://www.emergentmind.com/topics/attention-entropy-regularization) + [Attention Condensation 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.01564v1); ViT-S/CIFAR-100 75.0%@80% sparsity beats L1/L2, region-IoU 0.41→0.73; GPT-2 oracle-top65 CE 5.21→2.94 with condensation.
- **Plug-in pattern:** extra `consistency/separability/entropy/knowledge` terms end-to-end, no arch change; train-time masking (TargetDrop), EBAMA cross-attention alignment, AttReg mask-guided [Attention-Map-Reg survey](https://www.emergentmind.com/topics/attention-map-regularization); λ≫0.1 over-regularizes, ~3× BERT cost [EAR 2022].

## 4. KD-style focus (train-only teacher)
- **Logit KD:** soften + match `p_teacher→p_student`; weight-only effect [Hinton et al. 2015; PyTorch Tutorial](https://docs.pytorch.org/tutorials/beginner/knowledge_distillation_tutorial.html).
- **Dual-Head KD (fix for collapse):** `CE + BinaryKL` on one head degrades vs either alone — BinaryKL helps backbone but collapses classifier (neural-collapse/ETF view); split: main head CE, aux head BinaryKL; aux captures logit correlations best, dropped at test, little extra cost [Yang et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.08937).
- **Self-distillation (no external teacher):** aux lightweight neck aggregates shallow multi-scale features so distilled `cls+loc` supervision propagates through backbone; zero inference overhead [Tamura & Todo 2026](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0925231226011471); for tiny models (YOLO-N) down-weight aux for limited capacity.

## 5. Weighting strategies
- **Manual + schedule:** grid + decay/anneal; over-weight final exit if mean degrades it [Tang et al. 2023 via](https://www.emergentmind.com/topics/multi-exit-decoders-with-deep-supervision); BCAR two-stage `weak-count → joint·γ(t)` [BCAR-Net 2026](https://www.mdpi.com/2223-7747/15/12/1762).
- **Uncertainty weighting (Kendall et al. CVPR 2018):** homoscedastic `σ_i` per task, `L=Σ 1/(2σ_i²)L_i + log σ_i` (cls tempered variant); handles units/scales, beats singles [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.07115) ([CVPR](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2018/html/Kendall_Multi-Task_Learning_Using_CVPR_2018_paper.html)); caveat: `w∼1/L` can explode global LR [Chen et al. 2018]; ignores task correlation (depth↔normals) [IJCV 2025 review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11263-025-02625-x).
- **GradNorm (Chen et al. ICML 2018):** learn `w_i(t)` to match `‖∇_W w_iL_i‖ → Ḡ·[r_i]^α`, `r_i`=inverse training rate, renormalize `Σw_i=T`; α asymmetry; one-pass ≈ grid search, strong regularizer [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.02257) ([PMLR](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/chen18a/chen18a.pdf)).
- **Gradient surgery family:** see §6 when weighting alone insufficient (opposed directions).

## 6. Risks — gradient conflict & negative transfer
- **Diagnosis:** `cos(g_i,g_j)<0` + magnitude gap + high curvature = tragic triad → oscillation, slow, one task wins [Yu et al. NeurIPS 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.06782).
- **PCGrad:** if conflicting, `g_i←g_i − (g_i·g_j)/‖g_j‖²·g_j`; model-agnostic, ~1 extra backward per task; big gains on CIFAR-100 routing (71→77.5) + MT10/50 RL sample-efficiency [Yu et al. 2020](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2020/file/3fe78a8acf5fda99de95303940a2420c-Paper.pdf) ([survey 2026](https://www.emergentmind.com/topics/projecting-conflicting-gradients-pcgrad)).
- **Beyond:** CAGrad max-worst-improvement, MGDA Pareto convex combo, SAM-GS momentum, Ortho-LoRA bottleneck warning (low-rank forces collisions) [Ortho-LoRA 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.09684v1); channel-split shared/private to cut exit conflict [Gong et al. 2024]; under-parameterized self-aux towers for Pareto+generalization [MTL survey 2023](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.04959).
- **Aux-specific failures:** same-head CE+BinaryKL collapse → separate heads [DHKD 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.08937v1); recon mean-memorization/partial collapse, sensor-remap non-invariance [Voelcker et al. 2024]; entropy aux deleting right tokens [Nguyen et al. 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.12775v1); misaligned/deep aux over-regularizes [Deep Supervision survey](https://api.emergentmind.com/topics/deep-supervision); train cost +memory/compute hidden by inference pruning.
- **Practical guards:** monitor `cos(g_main,g_aux)`, grad-norm ratios, aux-vs-main curves; detach/schedule/down-weight aux in late phase; ablate (w/o recon, w/o depth, w/o entropy); keep aux decoders tiny; task grouping via conflict-graph coloring [2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.16959v1).

## TL;DR for time-series JEPA/TSAD
- Use **tiny recon decoder + 1D-YOLO interval head (BCE-obj + L1/1D-CIoU center/length + focal cls) + entropy/F-film sparsity** as *training-only* probes on clean + injected anomalies; weight with uncertainty→GradNorm, anneal aux to ~0 late; watch cosine-conflict, prune all aux at scoring.
